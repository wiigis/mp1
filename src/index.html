What is Fortnite?

Fortnite is a free-to-play online multiplayer game where players compete, explore, build, and create across a variety of game modes.

?

How It Works

Players can compete individually or with teammates in Solo, Duos, Trios, and Squads in various different gamemodes. In the Battle Royale gamemode, players compete to be the last player or team standing.

★

Why Play?

Fortnite combines competitive gameplay, teamwork, building, exploration, and constantly changing experiences in one online game.

Fortnite Items

Explore some useful healing and shield items

Mini Shield Potion

The Mini Shield Potion is a consumable item that restores a small amount of shield. It can be used quickly, making it useful during fights.

Shield Potion

The Shield Potion, commonly called a Big Shield, restores a larger amount of shield than a Mini Shield Potion, but takes longer to consume.

Slurp Juice

Slurp Juice is a consumable item that restores health and shield, making it useful for recovering after a battle.
